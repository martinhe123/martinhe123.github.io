A lodge shaped by its landscape

The Henry Horton State Park inn proposal connects hospitality with the Tennessee landscape. Developed by EOA Architects under the working title “Big Sky Lodge,” the design uses gabled roofs, stone, and expansive views to relate the new building to the park.

Guest rooms face the native grasslands, while shared outdoor spaces invite visitors to gather and spend time outside.

Design / Site & Arrival

Connecting the park buildings

The site plan links the new inn with the existing visitor center and restaurant. A revised entry drive organizes arrival, while the building form opens toward the landscape.
